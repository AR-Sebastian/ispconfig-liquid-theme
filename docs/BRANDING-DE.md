# Branding in LIQUID

LIQUID uses ISPConfig branding and a coordinated violet-blue accent system by default. Supported installations may replace:

- the logo for the light color scheme;
- the logo for the dark color scheme;
- the favicon;
- the accent color.

## Recommendations

- Use SVG or a transparent PNG for logos.
- Provide separate light and dark variants when contrast requires it.
- Keep the logo compact so navigation remains usable at smaller widths.
- Prefer saturated but controlled accent colors that remain legible on glass surfaces.
- Warning, error and success colors remain semantic and should not be replaced by the accent.
- Verify contrast on navigation, cards, forms, buttons and focus indicators.

Branding assets should not contain customer data or credentials. Keep a copy of the original files before replacing them.

# LIQUID 1.2.4

LIQUID 1.2.4 is a localization and visual-quality maintenance release for
ISPConfig 3.3.1p1. It reapplies translation after dynamically loaded views,
localizes generated table controls and hardens navigation contrast.

The standalone package completed 208 rendered desktop, tablet and mobile
acceptance states without contrast or surface failures. Historical OpenVZ,
APS Installer, Mailman 2 and XMPP surfaces remain outside the theme.
